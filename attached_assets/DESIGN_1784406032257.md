## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for a premium travel experience that balances high-utility planning with the thrill of exploration. The visual narrative combines **Modern Corporate** structure with **High-Contrast** energy. It utilizes a sophisticated "Night Sky" aesthetic—deep, technical foundations punctuated by vibrant, neon accents that mimic the glow of a city at night or the clarity of a mountain horizon.

The target audience consists of discerning travelers who value both efficiency and inspiration. The UI should feel exceptionally organized, reliable, and "pro-grade," while the use of vibrant mint and indigo accents provides a sense of forward-moving momentum and modern luxury.

## Colors

The palette is built on a high-contrast foundation. In **Light Mode (Light Grid)**, the interface uses cool grays and crisp whites to create a professional, "map-like" clarity. The Deep Charcoal text ensures maximum legibility for dense travel itineraries.

In **Dark Mode (Night Sky)**, the primary shifts to Neon Indigo, creating a high-tech, nocturnal feel. Across both modes, **Neon Mint (#00D8A4)** acts as the critical action color, reserved for primary conversion points, active states, and "adventure" triggers. Semantic colors (Success, Warning, Error) are highly saturated to ensure they remain visible against the deep backgrounds of the dark theme.

## Typography

This design system utilizes **Inter** exclusively to maintain a systematic, utilitarian aesthetic that emphasizes precision. 

- **Headlines:** Use tight letter-spacing and heavy weights (Bold/ExtraBold) to create a strong visual anchor for destination names and section headers.
- **Body:** Standardized at 16px for optimal readability during long-form guide reading.
- **Labels:** Small labels (12px-14px) should use medium or semi-bold weights with slight tracking (0.02em - 0.05em) for high-glanceability in data-heavy planning views.
- **Functional Caps:** Use all-caps sparingly for category tags or breadcrumbs to reinforce the "professional tool" feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by a **strict 4px grid system** to ensure a technical, precise appearance. 

- **Desktop:** A 12-column fluid grid with a 1280px max-width. Use 24px gutters to allow the high-contrast elements enough "air" to breathe.
- **Mobile:** A 4-column grid with 16px side margins. 
- **Content Blocks:** Use "Stack" patterns (vertical spacing) following the 4px scale. Components like cards should use `lg` (24px) padding to maintain a premium feel. 
- **Alignment:** All icons and text elements must snap to the 4px baseline to maintain the "Grid" design narrative.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain the "Modern & Clean" aesthetic, depth is achieved primarily through **Tonal Layering** and **Low-Contrast Outlines** rather than heavy shadows.

- **Surface Tiering:** Use `Surface Alt` for background sections to distinguish them from primary interactive cards (`Surface`).
- **Borders:** Every card and input field uses a 1px solid border (`Border` token). In Dark Mode, these borders are critical for defining shape against the "Night Sky" background.
- **Shadows:** Use only one "elevation" shadow for floating elements (modals, dropdowns). This shadow should be extremely diffused: `0px 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)` in light mode, and a tinted `0px 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)` in dark mode.
- **Active State:** Use a 2px stroke of the Accent color to indicate focus or selection, rather than an elevation change.

## Shapes

The design system uses a **Soft (0.25rem base)** corner radius to maintain a professional, architectural feel. 

- **Standard Elements:** Buttons, inputs, and small chips use the 4px (0.25rem) radius.
- **Large Containers:** Cards and modals use `rounded-lg` (8px / 0.5rem) to provide a subtle softening of the rigid grid.
- **Interactive Indicators:** While the system is generally geometric, progress bars and small status dots may use full "pill" rounding to distinguish them from structural UI components.

## Components

- **Buttons:** 
    - **Primary:** High-contrast (Primary color background, Text color in light mode / Accent color in dark mode). 
    - **Secondary:** Outlined with a 1px border.
    - **Adventure Action:** Background of `Accent` (Neon Mint) with `Primary` (Night Sky) text for high-urgency/premium actions.
- **Input Fields:** 1px border with `Surface Alt` background. On focus, the border transitions to `Accent` or `Indigo`.
- **Cards:** White or `Surface` background with a subtle 1px border. No shadow unless hovering. Images within cards should have a 0px top-radius if they bleed to the edge.
- **Chips/Tags:** Small, rectangular with 4px radius. Use `Primary Soft` background with `Text` color for a "technical" look.
- **Itinerary Timeline:** A vertical 2px line in `Border` color, with `Accent` color nodes to mark key travel milestones.
- **Lists:** High-density list items with 1px bottom dividers. Use `Label-MD` for metadata to maintain a clean, organized hierarchy.
